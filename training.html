Cloud Accounting Training

Practical accounting system training for nonprofit teams.

We help nonprofits, charities, NGOs, and PBOs confidently use cloud accounting platforms for grant tracking, donor reporting, fund accounting, and compliance.

TRAINING BUILT FOR NON PROFITS

Not generic software training - nonprofit focused financial systems training.

Many organisations invest in accounting software but struggle to configure it for restricted funds, grants, donors, projects, departments, and multi-currency reporting. Our training helps teams understand not just how to use the system, but how to use it correctly for nonprofit financial management.

Each session is practical, hands-on, and tailored to your organisation’s current systems, team capacity, and reporting needs.

Training Outcomes

  • Better grant tracking
  • Cleaner financial records
  • Stronger donor reporting
  • Confident finance teams
  • Audit-ready workflows
PLATFORMS WE TRAIN

Cloud accounting systems your nonprofit can actually use well.

We train teams on leading accounting platforms with nonprofit-specific setup, reporting, controls, and day-to-day usage.

QuickBooks Online

Training on nonprofit chart of accounts, classes, donors, grants, expenses, reports, and board-ready financial statements.

Xero

Learn fund tracking, project codes, multi-currency reporting, bank reconciliation, and nonprofit reporting workflows.

Odoo

Practical training for organisations using Odoo for accounting, operations, approvals, expense management, and reporting.

Zoho Books

Training on automation, expense tracking, budgets, donor reporting, approvals, and nonprofit-friendly financial processes.

Sage

Learn accounts production, nonprofit reporting, reconciliations, financial controls, and structured reporting workflows.
