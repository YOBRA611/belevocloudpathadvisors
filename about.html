OUR STORY

We believe every mission deserves strong financial foundations.

Belevo CloudPath Advisors was created with a simple belief: nonprofit organisations should have access to the same level of financial expertise as large corporations without the cost of maintaining a full in-house finance department.

As a fully virtual, cloud-first boutique financial consultancy, we work exclusively with nonprofits, charities, public benefit organisations, foundations, faith-based organisations, and NGOs. Unlike traditional accounting firms that serve every industry, our focus is entirely on mission-driven organisations.

Over the years, we have walked alongside organisations through donor audits, grant compliance reviews, financial system migrations, reporting challenges, and periods of organisational growth. These experiences have shaped our understanding of what nonprofits truly need: clarity, accountability, and trusted financial guidance.

Our name reflects who we are. Belevo represents our belief in creating value, while CloudPath represents the modern, technology-enabled pathway to financial excellence. Together, they embody our commitment to helping organisations strengthen their financial health so they can focus on creating lasting impact.
